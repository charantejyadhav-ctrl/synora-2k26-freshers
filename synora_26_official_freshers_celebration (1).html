About The Celebration

Welcoming the Incoming Batch to Kishkinda University

SYNORA '26 is the official freshers celebration organised by the Seniors of AIML Department at Kishkinda University. Built around the spirit of "A New Beginning. A New Experience.", it welcomes all 1st-year AI-ML and AI-DS students into campus life.

Organised By Seniors of AIML Department
Eligible Batch 1st-Year AI-ML & AI-DS Students
Celebration Highlights
  • High-energy outdoor & indoor sports including Box Cricket, Tug of War, and Carrom.
  • Mystery & strategy challenges with Hidden Chapters and Mind Maze.
  • Live cultural stage & creative events including Dance, Singing, Ramp Walk, Mono Act, Reel Making, and Free Fire.
